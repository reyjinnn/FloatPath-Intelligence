# Commercial Validation Plan — Datindo and Issuers

## Current factual state
Datindo publicly offers BAE, shareholder administration/analysis, and corporate actions (https://www.datindo.com/). **Unknown:** internal feature parity, budget, decision maker, procurement appetite. Public developer portfolio is not an official feature list. Sinartama Qaptive illustrates comparable paid analytics; not proof the same simulator is needed.

## Hypotheses to test
H1: Analysts spend measurable time reconciling ownership and scenarios. H2: provenance/dilution simulation is missing or painful. H3: BAE can package as premium service. H4: issuer teams may buy standalone even if BAE does not.

## Targets
Track discovery with Datindo + direct issuer Corporate Secretaries (only lawful contacts). Do not claim existing customer relationship or use Datindo brand/logo as endorsed.

## Outreach assets
- Neutral sandbox using synthetic entities.
- 60–90 sec narrated replay: import → verify → scenario → compare → memo.
- One-page validation sheet: rules scope, arithmetic tests, limitations, source URLs.
- Integration options (CSV, REST, white-label possible only with contractual permission).
- Short email requesting a **20-minute exploratory demo**, without listing unverified prices.

## Questions in a buyer conversation
1. How are free float/corporate action alternatives assessed today? Who owns review?
2. What is already automated/available to clients? Which existing tools?
3. Which cases require manual cross-checking and why? Time per case?
4. Would versioned evidence and scenario comparison fit a service offering?
5. What data can be supplied under lawful pilot terms? Data security constraints?
6. Who owns budget and vendor evaluation? What constitutes successful pilot?

## Pilot proposal structure
Duration e.g. 2–4 weeks after consent; 3–5 **authorized** cases (not promised real cases); human expert verification; time/corrections metrics; acceptance criteria; NDA/DPA; data retention; optional paid diagnostic decision after discussion.

## Funnel tracking
Date/contact role, channel, opened? (if consent/legal), reply, qualified meeting, problem evidence, objections, pilot commitment, lost reason. Silence from one prospect ≠ no market.

## Do not promise
A fixed budget of Rp200–450m, 50%+ probability of contract, exact integration with suspected private stack, automatic regulatory approval, investment outcomes, definitive legal compliance.
