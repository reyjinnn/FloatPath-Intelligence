# Cold Email and Demo Readiness (Datindo + Issuers)

## Audience segmentation
- Datindo: registrar business operations, product/service development, authorized IT decision-maker. Public corporate secretary mailbox may be initial routing point, not evidence of decision authority.
- Issuer: Corsec/IR team facing complex ownership planning. Avoid claiming every issuer is deficient or needs to buy the tool.

## First contact goals
- One clear problem and one 60–90s demo showing a hypothetical scenario.
- Ask only for a 20-minute workflow feedback discussion; no first-email pricing claims.
- Mention standalone, API-compatible, synthetic demo; never imply Datindo partnership.

## Readiness checklist
- [ ] Public demo is synthetic-only with clear watermark and no login.
- [ ] Video shows baseline 10%, scenario transfer/issuance, evidence and caveats.
- [ ] One-page validation sheet links tests and limitations.
- [ ] No confidential investor data in screenshot/video, analytics events, or source code.
- [ ] Demo and video links work on mobile/private browsing and require no download.
- [ ] Email subject is neutral; sender domain authentication configured where applicable.
- [ ] Reply/follow-up tracker records informed interest rather than opens alone.

## Example email (professional)
Subject: Modul simulasi free float & kesiapan aksi korporasi untuk layanan emiten

Yth. Tim Datindo Entrycom,

Saya mengembangkan FloatPath Intelligence, prototipe untuk membantu tim emiten memverifikasi data kepemilikan, membandingkan skenario pemenuhan target free float, dan menyiapkan memo analisis yang dapat ditelusuri ke dokumen sumber. Produk ini dirancang sebagai modul pelengkap layanan administrasi efek yang sudah berjalan, bukan pengganti sistem internal.

Berikut demo sintetis berdurasi singkat: [tautan video] dan [tautan prototipe]. Apakah proposal ini dapat diteruskan kepada tim yang menangani pengembangan layanan emiten untuk mendapat masukan dalam diskusi 20 menit?

Hormat saya,
[Nama pengirim]

## Follow-up/measurement
One reasonable follow-up after about a week; outreach to relevant issuer users in parallel. Record contact date, segment, qualified reply, actual demo, pilot scope discussion, objections, status. Do not infer product-market fit from silence.
