# Legal/Regulatory Review Checklist

Review target: official BEI Regulation I-A (31 March 2026 amendment), circular SE-00004/BEI/03-2026, applicable board rules, corporate-action regulations, and data usage terms.

- [ ] Obtain authentic official full text; record source URL, PDF hash, publication and effective dates.
- [ ] Extract exact section references for free-float definition and exclusions.
- [ ] Determine legal basis for 5% ownership threshold and possible approved exceptions.
- [ ] Confirm minimum number shares, SID threshold, measurement windows, permitted caveats.
- [ ] Confirm March 2027/2028/2029 transitional schedules with exact cohorts and dates.
- [ ] Review differences across listed boards, IPO vs continuing obligations, and special cases.
- [ ] Confirm whether beneficial ownership / affiliation facts are sufficient in public data.
- [ ] Review restrictions on market data, issuer report reproduction, brand/logo use.
- [ ] Review applicability of securities advice/consulting licensing to marketing and outputs.
- [ ] Review data protection, retention, subprocessor, international processing, and pilot contracts.
- [ ] Approve rules with reviewer name/date and golden-case reference.
- [ ] Document interpretation disagreements as unresolved, not hardcoded silently.
