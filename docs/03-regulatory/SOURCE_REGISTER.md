## How to promote a rule to VERIFIED
Attach official PDF/text copy with citation & page/section; record SHA-256, effective date, scope, reviewer identity, review date, test IDs, source license. If not possible, keep `DRAFT/NEEDS_LEGAL_REVIEW` and block regulated compliance claims.

## Evidence levels
`PRIMARY_VERIFIED` = official exact text checked; `PRIMARY_SUMMARY` = official announcement; `SECONDARY` = commentary; `HYPOTHESIS` = inferred business feature; `SYNTHETIC` = demo assumption.
